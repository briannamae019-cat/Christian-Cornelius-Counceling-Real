Ask us something else ↗
What does Christian counseling mean here?

It means your faith can be part of the conversation when it is meaningful to you. Counseling should respect your beliefs, questions, and preferences; you do not need to approach faith in one particular way to be welcomed.

What happens in the first session?

Your first conversation is an opportunity to share what prompted you to reach out and what kind of support you are looking for. It is also a chance to ask questions, learn about the counselor's approach, and consider whether the relationship feels like a good fit.

How do I know whether we're a good fit?

It is okay to ask about approach, experience, fees, and what working together might look like. A brief consultation can help you decide whether you feel comfortable with me and if we feel like this is a going to be a beneficial partnership for you.
